glory

Major Achievements

🏆

ODI World Cup

1983 (Kapil Dev) & 2011 (MS Dhoni) — Two historic ODI World Cup victories that defined generations of Indian cricket.

🥇

T20 World Cup

2007 (MS Dhoni) & 2024 (Rohit Sharma) — Back-to-back T20 titles cementing India as the greatest T20 nation.

🏅

Champions Trophy

2002, 2013 & 2025 — Three Champions Trophy titles including the iconic 2013 win under MS Dhoni's captaincy.

👑

World Test Championship

2023 — India's first WTC title defeating Australia in the final at The Oval, London.

🌏

Asia Cup

7 Asia Cup titles — Most successful team in Asia Cup history with dominant performances across all formats.

⭐

No.1 Test Ranking

India has spent the most time as ICC No.1 ranked Test team, reflecting consistent excellence across decades.
