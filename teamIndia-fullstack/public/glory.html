Kapil Dev's iconic 1983 miracle at Lord's against Clive Lloyd's West Indies, and MS Dhoni's legendary winning six at Wankhede in 2011 that fulfilled a billion dreams.

T20 World Cup

MS Dhoni's fearless young squad conquering Johannesburg in 2007, and Rohit Sharma's invincible 2024 campaign in Barbados cementing timeless T20 royalty.

Joint-winners in Colombo 2002 under Sourav Ganguly, Dhoni's rain-curtailed defensive masterclass at Edgbaston in 2013, and continued dominance in the mini-World Cup.

3× Champions
